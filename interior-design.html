General terms

    
  1. Rates are applicable to the specified scope and specifications only.
  2. Final quantities will be measured as per approved drawings/design.
  3. Colour, laminate, hardware and finish selections are subject to package limits.
  4. Any change in specification or upgrade requested by the client will be charged additionally.
  5. Civil, electrical, plumbing, painting, false ceiling, glass, mirror, appliances and decorative works are excluded unless specifically mentioned.
  6. Additional/fancy works will be charged separately based on actual requirement.
  7. Final design and material selections shall be approved before commencement of production.
  8. Rates are intended as package rates and may be revised where the client selects specialty materials, imported hardware or customized finishes.
  9. All dimensions and quantities shall be finalized based on the approved working drawings.
